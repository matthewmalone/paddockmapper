D draw · click to place corners · click the first corner, double-click or Enter to close · Backspace removes the last corner · Esc stops.

M measure · click points to measure a distance; click the first point to close it and get an area · double-click or Enter ends a line · Elevation shows the ground profile along it.

Lock a paddock with the padlock on its card: its corners can't be moved or deleted, and it can't be deleted or cleared until unlocked. Names and colours stay editable. Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo.

E edit · drag a corner to move it (shared corners move together) · drag a small midpoint dot to add a corner · right-click or Delete removes a corner · hold Alt while dragging to separate a shared corner from the selected paddock.

Elevation profiles use SRTM 30 m satellite elevation data (Open Topo Data), falling back to Copernicus 90 m (Open-Meteo). They show the lie of the land well, but individual heights can be out by a few metres, so they are for planning, not survey levels.

Lengths and bearings are on the WGS84 ellipsoid (Vincenty), bearings are true. MGA coordinates use GRS80; GDA2020 and WGS84 positions agree to within about 1–2 m, which is less than typical imagery error.
